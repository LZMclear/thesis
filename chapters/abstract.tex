\begin{abstract}
去蜂窝大规模多输入多输出 (Cell-Free Massive Multiple-Input Multiple-Output, CF-mMIMO) 系统通过密集部署大量接入点 (Access Point, AP)，打破了传统蜂窝网络的小区边界限制，实现了多点协作传输与无缝通信覆盖。得益于有利的传播条件，CF-mMIMO能够获取更丰富的宏分集增益，显著提升用户频谱效率与系统总容量，同时有效抑制小区间干扰。然而，该架构在实际部署中面临高昂的成本压力，且不可避免地引入非理想硬件因素。本文针对上述挑战，系统研究了非理想硬件条件下CF-mMIMO系统的资源分配与性能优化问题。

针对CF-mMIMO中巨大的硬件损伤和损耗问题，本文首先构建了非线性功率放大器 (Power Amplifier, PA) 与低分辨率模数转换器 (Analog-to-Digital Converter, ADC) 联合作用下的上行传输方案，其次对上行用户可达速率性能指标进行了推导。同时，分析了AP数量、UE密度、AP天线数目及ADC分辨率等关键参数对系统性能的影响。此外，为了最大化系统和速率，提出了基于功率控制的资源分配优化问题。然而，由于目标函数的非凸特性，通过考虑UE间干扰图结构特征，提出了图神经网络 (Graph Neural Network, GNN) 辅助的演员-评论家 (Actor-Critic) 算法框架来对问题进行了有效地解决。进一步地，为了最小化量化噪声影响，设计了多智能体深度Q集成网络 (Multi-Agent Deep Q-Integration Network, MADQIN) 算法以优化ADC分辨率分配策略。

此外，本文进一步研究了非理想硬件条件下CF-mMIMO系统的超可靠低时延通信 (Ultra-Reliable Low-Latency Communication, URLLC) 服务资源分配策略。针对工业自动化等物联网关键应用对毫秒级时延与极高可靠性的严苛需求，构建了非线性PA的损伤模型，推导了有限块长 (Finite Blocklength, FBL) 下上行链路UE速率闭合表达式。为降低传统优化算法的计算复杂度，提出基于GNN与DRL融合的智能功率控制策略，通过图嵌入技术建模UE空间相关性，动态抑制硬件损伤与时变信道干扰。所提方案在保持毫秒级时延的同时，为工业自动化等物联网应用提供理论与技术支撑。仿真结果充分验证了所提方案的有效性。

\keywords{去蜂窝大规模多输入多输出系统; 非理想硬件; 图神经网络; 深度强化学习; 功率分配; 超可靠低时延通信; 有限块长; 资源分配}

\end{abstract}

\begin{englishabstract}
Cell-free massive multiple-input multiple-output (CF-mMIMO) systems, characterized by dense deployment of access points (APs), eliminate the conventional cell boundary limitations of traditional cellular networks and enable multi-point cooperative transmission with seamless communication coverage. Benefiting from favorable propagation conditions, CF-mMIMO can achieve more abundant macro-diversity gains, significantly enhancing user spectral efficiency and overall system capacity while effectively suppressing inter-cell interference. However, the practical implementation of this architecture faces substantial cost challenges and inevitably introduces non-ideal hardware impairments. This dissertation systematically addresses resource allocation and performance optimization challenges in CF-mMIMO systems operating under non-ideal hardware conditions.

To address the significant hardware impairments and losses in CF-mMIMO, this research first constructs an uplink transmission scheme incorporating nonlinear power amplifiers (PAs) and low-resolution analog-to-digital converters (ADCs). Subsequently, closed-form expressions for the uplink user achievable rate performance metric are derived. Meanwhile, the impacts of key parameters such as AP quantity, UE density, AP antenna number, and ADC resolution on system performance are analyzed. Furthermore, to maximize the system sum rate, a resource allocation optimization problem based on power control is formulated. However, due to the non-convex nature of the objective function, a graph neural network (GNN)-assisted actor-critic algorithmic framework is proposed by considering the UE interference graph structural features to effectively solve the problem. Additionally, to minimize the quantization noise impact, a multi-agent deep Q-integration network (MADQIN) algorithm is designed to optimize the ADC resolution allocation strategy.

In addition, this research extends to resource allocation strategies for ultra-reliable low-latency communication (URLLC) services in CF-mMIMO systems with hardware impairments. Addressing the stringent requirements of millisecond-level latency and ultra-high reliability in critical Internet of Things applications such as industrial automation, impairment models for nonlinear PAs are established, and closed-form rate expressions for uplink UEs under finite blocklength (FBL) constraints are derived. To reduce the computational complexity of traditional optimization algorithms, an intelligent power control strategy integrating GNN with DRL is developed. By leveraging graph embedding techniques to model spatial correlations among UEs, the proposed approach enables dynamic suppression of hardware-induced distortions and time-varying channel interference. The developed solution maintains millisecond-level latency while providing theoretical and technical support for IoT applications in industrial automation. Simulation results comprehensively validate the effectiveness of the proposed methodologies.

\englishkeywords{CF-mMIMO, non-ideal hardware, graph neural network, deep reinforcement learning, power allocation, ultra-reliable low-latency communication, finite blocklength, resource allocation}

\end{englishabstract}
